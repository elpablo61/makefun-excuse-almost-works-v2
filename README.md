One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — 100 characters, 18 words, one line of plain text:

> Boss, my boiler burst at 6am, the flat's ankle-deep and the emergency plumber can't get here till 2.

Why this line: a tired manager has heard every excuse twice. This one is boring, specific and home-bound — a burst boiler at 6am, ankle-deep water, an emergency plumber who can't come till 2. No wild story, no setup, no explanation, no signature. Just a text sent in a hurry.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
